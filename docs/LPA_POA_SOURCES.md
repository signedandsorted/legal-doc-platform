# Sources for LPA presets, business guidance and ordinary powers of attorney

All operative wording in this work is taken from the public sources below. It is **draft** and is **not legal advice**. A solicitor (England and Wales) must review it before a client signs anything. Checked on 18 November 2026.

## Lasting powers of attorney — preferences and instructions

- **LP12** (authoritative official guide): [Make and register your lasting power of attorney: a guide (web version)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/lasting-power-of-attorney-forms/lp12-make-and-register-your-lasting-power-of-attorney-a-guide-web-version), Office of the Public Guardian, updated **3 November 2026**. Open Government Licence v3.0.
  - Definitions of preferences and instructions, and the warning that complicated or badly worded wording can make an LPA unworkable.
  - Example preferences and instructions for property and financial affairs LPAs and health and welfare LPAs (used as the tick-box presets).
  - Professional attorney fee instruction: “I wish my professional attorneys to be paid the standard solicitor rate as set by [state the name of a relevant professional organisation here].”
  - Gift limits, invalid “must make gifts / maintain others” instructions, and the advice that attorneys must already consult family and friends when considering best interests (section 8 / Mental Capacity Act).
  - Special case: two LP1F forms, with the official personal-only and business-only instructions.
  - You can appoint different attorneys for personal finances and business affairs by filling in two LP1F forms.
  - Discretionary investment management: current LP12 does **not** print a standard clause. It says you should consider taking legal advice on whether specific provision is necessary, because at least one major financial institution has required an express instruction.
- **LPA forms**: [GOV.UK lasting power of attorney forms](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/lasting-power-of-attorney-forms) (LP1F, LP1H and related).
- **Avoiding errors**: [Avoiding errors when completing a lasting power of attorney form](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/avoiding-errors-when-completing-a-lasting-power-of-attorney-form/avoiding-errors-when-completing-a-lasting-power-of-attorney-form).
- **GOV.UK LPA overview**: [Make, register or end a lasting power of attorney](https://www.gov.uk/power-of-attorney) (registration still described as 8 to 10 weeks if there are no mistakes).

### Discretionary investments / stocks and shares (former OPG clause)

The tick-box wording beginning “My attorneys may transfer my investments into a discretionary management scheme…” is **not** in the current LP12. It is the former OPG “standard clause X” that was printed in LP12 until March 2022.

Source for the exact wording, and for the fact that OPG later removed it: Sheree Green, [“A good investment”](https://communities.lawsociety.org.uk/may-2022/a-good-investment/6002319.article), The Law Society, 12 May 2022. The article quotes the old clause and the replacement LP12 advice (which still appears in the 3 November 2026 LP12).

The app offers that former wording because some financial institutions still ask for an express instruction about discretionary management and nominee holdings. A property and financial affairs LPA already covers making or selling investments. A solicitor must review this option. It is clearly labelled as not being in the current official guide.

## Ordinary / general and specific powers of attorney

- [Powers of Attorney Act 1971, section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/27/section/10) — effect of a general power in the specified form (or a form to the like effect expressed to be made under the Act), and the trustee / personal representative / Settled Land Act limits, as modified by the Trustee Delegation Act 1999.
- [Powers of Attorney Act 1971, Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/27/schedule/1) — the statutory form of a general power of attorney.
- [Powers of Attorney Act 1971, section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1971/27/section/1) — a power of attorney must be executed as a deed by the donor.
- [Law of Property (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1989, section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/34/section/1) — execution of deeds (signature in the presence of a witness who attests).
- [HM Land Registry Practice Guide 9](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/powers-of-attorney-and-registered-land/practice-guide-9-powers-of-attorney-and-registered-land) — a general power under section 10; other general or special (limited) powers may use any wording but must be executed as a deed; death, bankruptcy or mental incapacity of the donor automatically revokes the power; the donor may revoke it at any time; extra care when the power will be used for registered land.

A specific (limited) power has no statutory form. The draft deed in this app only names the parties, states the limited purpose in the client’s words, states that it is not a section 10 general power, and is executed as a deed. A solicitor must review it.

## Business assets in wills

- [Business Relief for Inheritance Tax](https://www.gov.uk/business-relief-inheritance-tax)
- [What qualifies for Business Relief](https://www.gov.uk/business-relief-inheritance-tax/what-qualifies-for-business-relief)

GOV.UK currently uses the name **Business Relief** (often still called business property relief). The in-app summary is descriptive only. In particular, for deaths on or after 6 April 2026, 100% relief is capped at £2.5 million of qualifying business or agricultural property, with 50% relief on the value above that cap. A solicitor must advise on a particular estate. The will does not invent operative tax or trust wording; it leaves a solicitor-drafting placeholder.

## What a solicitor should especially check

- Every ticked section 7 instruction, especially professional fees (including the organisation named), former discretionary-investment clause, “must not sell my home”, “must not move into residential care”, and any gift wording.
- Business / personal split instructions on two LP1F forms, and any partnership or shareholders’ agreement.
- Will placeholders for business or agricultural assets and Business Relief, including the 6 April 2026 cap.
- The ordinary general power (Schedule 1 “to the like effect” filling, attestation, and any extra end date).
- The specific limited power in full, especially if it will be used for land or registered title.
